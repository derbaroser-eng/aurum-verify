Try it with curl

curl -s -X POST https://cutting-oclc-studio-theology.trycloudflare.com/v1/verify/claims \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"claims":["AI will replace all jobs"],"sources":[{"url":"https://ourworldindata.org/ai-jobs"}],"minimum_sources":1}'
